Selected work

MediProof

Health misinformation fact-checker

Checks a health claim from text, a voice note or a photo of an infographic, then answers with citations from WHO, CDC, PubMed, NIH and Cochrane.

  • Retrieval-augmented pipeline built on n8n and Gemini 2.5 Flash
  • Answers translated into nine languages, including Malay, Chinese and Tamil
  • Chrome extension: highlight a claim on TikTok, X, Facebook or WhatsApp Web and check it in one click

Lead Developer, Team 110011. Champion at Sedia! 2026, built for UN SDG 3.

View repository on GitHub

SPENZER

Smart medicine dispenser for elder care

Identifies pills with a camera and hands them out from a motor-driven carousel, with a web interface for monitoring.

  • Custom YOLO pill-detection model, deployed as ONNX and TensorFlow Lite
  • ESP32-S3 with motor control running the dispensing carousel
  • WebSocket link carrying device commands and telemetry to the backend

Built for the UTM AI Showcase.
